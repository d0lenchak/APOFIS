Where does the Summary information come from?
Everything is linked by p-code: PXXX YYYY-ZZZZ/ZZZ/.ZZZ
PXXX links all financials via project code, and YYYY-ZZZZ/ZZZ/.ZZZ links all operationals via study ID

CDU: [CDU] of selected CDU from FULL_MAP.xlsx
Target: [Program] of selected Program from FULL_MAP.xlsx
Indication: [Indication] of selected Study from Study Daily Report.xlsx
Program: [Primary] of selected Program from FULL_MAP.xlsx
Study/Code: [Account?..] of selected Program from CORE_Actuals/Forecast.xlsx
Study-Specific Codes: count of actual studies within a Program, based on Hyperion Code syntax (..-1231 vs Gen.Gen..)

Phase: [Phase] of selected Study from Study Daily Report.xlsx
Status: [Operational Status (COMPASS)] of selected Study from Study Daily Report.xlsx
Enrollment:[# Enrollment (Actual) / # Enrollment (Planned] of selected Study from Study Daily Report.xlsx
